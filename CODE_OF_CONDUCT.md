# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. Disagree about code, not people. No harassment, personal
attacks, or discriminatory language, here or in linked spaces (issues, PRs,
discussions).

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that don't meet
this, and may block anyone whose behavior is disruptive or harmful.

To report a problem, open a GitHub issue or contact the maintainer
([@tuvo1106](https://github.com/tuvo1106)) directly.
